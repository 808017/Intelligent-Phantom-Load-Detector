# Current Sensor Calibration

## SCT-013-000

The current measurement circuit uses the SCT-013-000
non-invasive current transformer.

For the selected burden resistor:

Primary current = 30 A

Secondary current ≈ 16.13 mA RMS

With 40 Ω burden:

V = 16.13 mA × 40 Ω
  ≈ 0.645 V RMS

Therefore:

Scaling factor ≈ 0.645 V / 30 A
               ≈ 0.0215 V/A
